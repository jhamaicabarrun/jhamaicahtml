Jhamaica Barrun Recipe for the Fried Rice

Ingredients

For the fried rice

  • oil
  • garlic
  • ginisa
  • salt
  • onion
  • black pepper
  • msg

Instructions

  1. Heat the pan.
  2. Put the oil.
  3. Stir fry the garlic and onions.
  4. Pour the rice
  5. Add the salt and the msgas a seasoning.
  6. Sprinkle the black pepper for additional taste.
  7. wait for the seasonings to soak
